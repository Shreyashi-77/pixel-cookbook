ITALIAN RECIPE

CHEESY PIZZA

Warm melty pizza slices with dreamy café night vibes ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Pizza toppings

STEPS

  1. Roll out dough
  2. Spread tomato sauce
  3. Add cheese & toppings
  4. Bake until golden
  5. Slice carefully
  6. Serve hot 🍕
