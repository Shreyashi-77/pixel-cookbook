PASTA RECIPE

COZY SPAGHETTI

Comfort pasta night with rich tomato sauce and pixel café vibes ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Cheese

STEPS

  1. Boil pasta
  2. Cook garlic in oil
  3. Add tomato sauce
  4. Mix pasta gently
  5. Add cheese on top
  6. Serve warm 🍝
