BAKERY RECIPE

STRAWBERRY SHORTCAKE

Fluffy cake layers with strawberries and whipped cream ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Whipped cream
  • Fresh strawberries

STEPS

  1. Bake cake layers
  2. Whip fresh cream
  3. Slice strawberries
  4. Layer cake carefully
  5. Add cream & berries
  6. Chill before serving 🍰
