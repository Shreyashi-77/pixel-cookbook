BREAKFAST RECIPE

FLUFFY PANCAKES

Soft buttery pancake stacks with cozy café vibes ✨ Perfect for sleepy mornings and strawberry milk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Vanilla extract ✨

STEPS

  1. Mix dry ingredients
  2. Add milk, egg & butter
  3. Whisk until smooth
  4. Cook on medium heat
  5. Flip when bubbles appear
  6. Serve with syrup 🍯
