BREAD RECIPE

ROSEMARY FOCACCIA

Golden fluffy focaccia with herbs and cozy bakery vibes ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • Bread flour
  • Olive oil
  • Yeast
  • Rosemary
  • Sea salt

STEPS

  1. Prepare dough
  2. Let dough rise
  3. Stretch into tray
  4. Add rosemary & oil
  5. Bake until golden
  6. Serve warm 🍞
