BAKERY RECIPE

CHOCOLATE COOKIES

Tiny warm cookie bites with chocolate chip happiness ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Brown sugar
  • Chocolate chips
  • Vanilla extract

STEPS

  1. Mix butter & sugar
  2. Add dry ingredients
  3. Fold chocolate chips
  4. Shape cookie dough
  5. Bake until golden
  6. Cool before serving 🍪
