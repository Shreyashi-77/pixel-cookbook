FRENCH BAKERY

BUTTERY CROISSANT

Golden flaky layers with soft Paris café morning vibes ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Yeast
  • Milk
  • Sugar

STEPS

  1. Prepare dough
  2. Layer with butter
  3. Fold several times
  4. Shape croissants
  5. Bake until golden
  6. Serve warm 🥐
