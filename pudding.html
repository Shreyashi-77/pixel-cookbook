DESSERT RECIPE

CARAMEL PUDDING

Soft silky pudding with dreamy caramel sweetness ✨

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • Vanilla extract
  • Caramel syrup

STEPS

  1. Heat milk gently
  2. Whisk eggs & sugar
  3. Combine mixtures
  4. Pour into moulds
  5. Steam or bake softly
  6. Chill before serving 🍮
